# Visual Reference Study

Reference reviewed on 2026-10-21:
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E7zy4fu5WIE>

## What the reference does

- Runs for 4:56 and presents five short A2 conversations as continuous scenes.
- Shows one current utterance at a time in a large, high-contrast yellow caption area.
- Keeps the text in the lower-centre/centre of the frame with generous margins.
- Changes the background scene when the situation changes, rather than using presentation slides.
- Uses roughly 18–20 turns per scenario at natural conversational speed.
- Does not teach translations, pronunciation, or guided repetition before the conversations.

## Talkivana adaptation

- Borrow only the readable sentence orientation and continuous-scene feeling; do not copy imagery,
  wording, characters, branding, or artwork.
- During teaching, keep English above German in one stable scene. Speak English once, then German
  twice without changing to a separate slide.
- Keep simplified pronunciation below the German sentence.
- End with the complete coherent conversation in German only, with each line spoken exactly once.
- Do not append a repeated lesson bundle after the German-only ending.
- Use the owner-approved rich geometric templates: teal, berry, midnight/gold, and sunset/navy.
  Keep one template consistent within a video and rotate templates across daily videos.

## Competitive opportunity

The reference is strong at immediacy and readability but is primarily listening practice.
Talkivana should differentiate through a clear guided-learning phase followed by an uninterrupted
German comprehension pass, while preserving the reference's uncluttered text hierarchy.

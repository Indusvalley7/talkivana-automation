Review contract

What each approval means

The two approvals are not interchangeable. The second approval never excuses content that should have been corrected at the first gate.

APPROVAL 1

Content is teachable

  • Natural Standard German
  • Accurate English meaning
  • Complete scenario and stable roles
  • Useful tutor guidance
  • Correct Short-to-long vocabulary link
APPROVAL 2

The exact media is publishable

  • Voices and speakers are correct
  • No missing or extra narration
  • Pacing and pauses feel right
  • Text is readable and synchronized
  • Title, description, and thumbnail fit

Publishing clock

A stable four-slot conveyor

Every future asset has a fixed role. Daily packs are prepared one day ahead, and consecutive packs overlap so the channel receives one publication approximately every 4½–5½ hours during the active day.

OWNER LOCKED

No silent rotation

Analytics can recommend a change, but cannot rearrange slots automatically.

Existing schedules stay exactly as they are

The conveyor applies only when a future pack receives final approval. Videos already public, private, or scheduled on YouTube are not migrated by this change.

Missed-slot policy

Catch up without creating a burst

An approved asset should not wait a full day just because its assigned window passed during corrections.

Slot is still ahead

Keep the planned time and preserve the experiment assignment.

Slot has passed

Schedule near-immediate publication with a five-minute API buffer.

Several assets missed

Space catch-up publications by at least 30 minutes; never release all four together.

Analytics treatment

Mark catch-up assets separately so an abnormal publication time does not contaminate the normal slot experiment.

“Successfully published” has a strict meaning

The tracker changes this field to “yes” only after YouTube reports the asset as public. Approval, scheduling, or a private upload alone is not publication.

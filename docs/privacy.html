Privacy policy

Talkivana Automation · Effective 23 October 2026

About this application

Talkivana Automation is a privately operated tool used by the owner of the Talkivana YouTube channel. It helps create, schedule, upload, and evaluate language-learning videos. It is not offered as a public service to third parties.

Google data the application accesses

After the channel owner grants permission, the application may access:

  • YouTube channel, playlist, and video information;
  • permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's channel; and
  • read-only YouTube Analytics reports used to improve publishing times and content.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to operate the Talkivana content workflow, publish approved videos, organize them into playlists, and assess their performance. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are not committed to the public source repository. Workflow logs and generated artifacts may be retained according to the repository's GitHub Actions retention settings. Access credentials are retained until they are replaced or revoked by the channel owner.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the application's Google access at any time from the Google Account connections page.

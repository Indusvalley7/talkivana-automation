Primary production path

From timer to public video

The daily path is intentionally asynchronous. Content review may take hours without keeping a machine alive; a comment event wakes only the next relevant stage.

1. Generate draft

A schedule or manual dispatch checks the automation switch and seven-pack backlog cap, generates one linked pack, validates it, opens an Issue, and updates the tracker.

2. Process content approval

An owner comment is parsed from its first non-empty line. Revision stays in the light text stage; approval launches production.

3. Fan out media workers

Four asset jobs prepare voice and visuals separately, then final assembly and QA produce two Shorts and two long videos.

4. Upload privately

The exact approved candidates go to YouTube as private. The Issue receives private links; GitHub artifacts expose only short review clips.

5. Process final approval

The second approval applies the publishing plan. If a slot has already passed, the catch-up policy can publish shortly after approval.

6. Observe and learn

Tracker and timing jobs collect comparable 24-hour and 7-day evidence. They report findings without silently rearranging the owner-approved conveyor.

Observability

Where to watch a run

The Action graph is the live process view. The Issue is the approval console. The tracker is the historical view. Each answers a different question.

LIVE

Actions graph

Shows queued, running, successful, skipped, and failed jobs—including which parallel asset worker failed.

DECISIONS

Pack Issue

Shows lesson content, corrections, labels, preview links, and the owner command that moved state.

HISTORY

Tracker

Shows approval reason, scheduled time in IST, publication outcome, IDs, and comparable analytics.

Failure behavior

Stop safely, then resume narrowly

The pipeline does not hide a partial failure by continuing. It records what finished and retries only the necessary boundary.

Bad contentSTOP No rendering or upload.
Voice failureRETRY STAGE Rebuild voice-dependent output.
Partial uploadRESUME Reuse manifested video IDs; do not duplicate.
Schedule failurePRIVATE Video remains non-public until corrected.
Missing secretSETUP BLOCK Report the exact credential category.

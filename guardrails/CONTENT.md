# Talkivana Content Rules

- Create exactly two vocabulary Shorts and two linked conversation lessons in every pack.
- Every Short contains only Standard German words or verbs, their English meanings, part of speech,
  and simplified English pronunciation. No example sentences belong in a Short.
- Every long video contains complete conversation lines and sentences, not a grammar lecture.
- Each Short's vocabulary must appear verbatim in its linked conversation.
- The Short is scheduled one day before the linked conversation.
- Use English first, then Standard German, then repeat the same German naturally.
- Keep English above German in the teaching view, with simplified pronunciation below German.
- Never speak or spell out the simplified pronunciation guide. It is a visual aid only.
- End each long lesson with the complete coherent conversation in German only, spoken once in natural order.
- Give every character in the final conversation a distinct fixed approved voice. If there are more
  characters than approved voices, stop before rendering instead of reusing or inventing a voice.
- Never stretch or slow German audio in a way that changes pronunciation.
- Use Standard German used in Germany. Do not use dialect spellings or regional dialect lessons.
- Build progressive, original, practical situations for absolute beginners.
- Make each scenario, objective, wording, and learning progression materially distinct.
- Avoid recent vocabulary unless deliberate review is named and pedagogically useful.
- Do not include URLs, purchases, engagement bait, claims of fluency, or calls to subscribe.
- Avoid generic or interchangeable mass-produced lessons.

# Standard German Quality Gate

Before presenting a content pack:

1. Verify Standard German spelling, capitalisation, grammar, gender, case, verb forms, and register.
2. Verify every English translation preserves the German meaning and tone.
3. Verify every simplified pronunciation is understandable to an English speaker and is not IPA.
4. Verify each conversation is coherent, natural, useful, and suitable for an absolute beginner.
5. Verify both speakers participate and that the dialogue has a clear situational progression.
6. Verify every linked Short term appears in its conversation.
7. Verify every teaching sentence is spoken English once, German once, then German repeated once.
8. Verify the final section is the complete coherent conversation, spoken in German only once; do not append English or another repetition after it.
9. Replace uncertain language with simpler language rather than guessing.
10. Verify the two scenarios are materially different and not a cosmetic template variation.
11. Return structured data only when a schema is requested.
12. Verify tutor guidance is useful and varied rather than repeating an identical filler phrase.
13. Keep learner response time proportional to sentence length and never longer than six seconds.
14. Confirm no narration transcript contains the simplified pronunciation spelling.
15. Confirm final-conversation speaker labels map one-to-one to fixed approved reference voices.
